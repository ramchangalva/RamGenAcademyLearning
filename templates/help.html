Understanding AI Recommendations

STRONG_BUY / BUY

Multiple positive indicators suggest this is a good buying opportunity. Consider entering a position.

  • Strong technical signals
  • Positive fundamentals
  • Bullish price predictions
  • High confidence score (70-100%)
HOLD

Mixed signals suggest waiting for clearer direction. Maintain current position or wait before trading.

  • Neutral technical signals
  • Mixed fundamentals
  • Uncertain price direction
  • Medium confidence (40-70%)
STRONG_SELL / SELL

Multiple negative indicators suggest reducing or exiting position. Consider selling or avoiding.

  • Bearish technical signals
  • Weak fundamentals
  • Negative price predictions
  • High confidence (70-100%)

Understanding Technical Indicators

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

Range: 0-100

  • Above 70: Overbought - Potential sell signal
  • Below 30: Oversold - Potential buy signal
  • 30-70: Neutral zone
MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

Components: MACD line, Signal line, Histogram

  • MACD above Signal: Bullish momentum
  • MACD below Signal: Bearish momentum
  • Crossover: Potential trend change
EMA 20/50/200 Alert System

Exponential Moving Averages: EMA 20 (green), EMA 50 (blue), EMA 200 (red)

  • Price above EMA 200: Long-term uptrend
  • Price below EMA 200: Long-term downtrend
  • EMA 20 crosses EMA 50: Short-term trend change
  • Golden Cross: EMA 20/50 above EMA 200 (very bullish)
  • Death Cross: EMA 20/50 below EMA 200 (very bearish)
Bollinger Bands

Components: Upper band, Middle band (SMA), Lower band

  • Price touches upper band: Overbought
  • Price touches lower band: Oversold
  • Band width: Indicates volatility
